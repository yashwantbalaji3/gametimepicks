# Open Questions — what I need to become a better VP

**Maintained by:** Claude (VP of Product & Operations) · **Updated:** 2026-07-06

These are ranked by how much the answer would change my recommendations. Answer any, ignore the rest — I'll fold your answers into `KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md`.

## Tier 1 — reshapes prioritization
1. **What is the real goal of GameTime Picks?** Portfolio showcase / audience growth / a validated model / eventual monetization / personal tool? This single answer changes everything downstream.
2. **Who is the primary user today, and who do you *want* it to be?** Recruiters, sports fans, yourself, or a future paying audience?
3. **What does "success" look like in 3 months?** One concrete metric (traffic, hit rate on a curated subset, a launch milestone, a job offer).

## Tier 2 — fills real gaps
4. **Is the public README intentionally kept in "demo portfolio" framing, or is it just stale?** (It contradicts the live product.)
5. **What's the current settlement performance** since late May? (My numbers are from `METHODOLOGY_FINDINGS_2026-05-22.md`.)
6. **What breaks most often** in the daily loop — provider timeouts, settlement gaps, deploy issues, something else?
7. **How autonomous do you want it?** Is `ENABLE_AUTONOMOUS_DEPLOY` on? Do you want to review every deploy or trust the gates?

## Tier 3 — nice to have
8. Are there analytics on the live site (traffic, page engagement)? If not, do you want them?
9. Budget constraints on paid providers / Odds API credits?
10. Any legal/compliance guidance you're working under (beyond the responsible-use framing)?
