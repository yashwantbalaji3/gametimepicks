# `vp/` — VP of Product & Operations workspace

Permanent home for GameTime Picks strategy, product management, and institutional memory — maintained by Claude as your VP of Product & Operations. Contains **no product code** and never modifies the repo. Output = knowledge and implementation plans for Claude Code.

## Start here
- **`KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md`** — the single source of truth (currently **v0.2**). Confirmed-current state vs. historical context, cleanly split.
- **`decisions/0001-OPEN-STRATEGIC-DECISIONS.md`** — the 10 decisions I need from you now.
- **`plans/NEXT-FOR-CLAUDE-CODE.md`** — sequenced pre-launch work for Claude Code.
- **`MISSING_DOCS.md`** — prioritized documentation gaps.
- **`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`** — lower-level open questions.
- **`ops/OPERATING_CADENCE.md`** — your daily/weekly/monthly founder rhythm.
- **`inbox.md`** — drop raw notes anytime; I process them into the KB.

## How we work (from the founding briefing)
Mission loop: (1) audit current state → (2) break into departments → (3) parallel reviews → (4) merge findings → (5) prioritize by impact × effort × risk → (6) tell you what Claude Code should implement. I challenge assumptions and offer better alternatives; I don't stop at problems. I produce the plan; Code implements; nothing is "done" until the gates prove it.

Product Management · UI/UX · Quantitative Research · QA · Data Engineering · Operations · Launch Management · Customer Experience.

## Freshness rule
Newest Claude Code deployment reports and current repo artifacts outrank older handoffs and the README. Newest source wins on conflict.
