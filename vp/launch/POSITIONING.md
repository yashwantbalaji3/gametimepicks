# Launch Positioning (one-pager)

**Maintained by:** Claude (VP) · **v1 — 2026-07-06** · per ADR-0002, ADR-0004, ADR-0010

## Positioning statement
For **sports fans who want trustworthy picks without the homework**, GameTime Picks is a **paper-only sports analytics product** that gives model-backed picks and tracks every single result in the open. Unlike tipster accounts and pick-sellers that hide their losses and promise locks, GameTime Picks **shows its whole record and never touches real money** — the sportsbook feel, with none of the dishonesty.

## One-liner
*"Model-backed sports picks with nothing hidden — every pick explained, every result tracked, all on paper."*

## Brand voice
- **Honest, plain, confident-but-never-certain.** We say "the model leans," "here's the risk," "no play tonight" — never "lock," "guaranteed," "easy money."
- **Sportsbook-*inspired* look, responsible copy.** Vault/gold, cards, ladders, a bit of showmanship in the *visuals*; disciplined, caveated language in the *words*. The tension is intentional and on-brand.
- **Show the misses.** A visible 0–17 longshot record is a feature, not a bug — it's the proof we don't hide.

## Messaging pillars
1. **Nothing hidden** — every pick has a reason, a risk, and a source; every result settles in public.
2. **Paper-only, always** — no real-money betting, no wallet, no affiliate links. Educational and honest.
3. **A real track record** — a canonical bankroll settled only on official finals, forensically audited.
4. **Fresh daily** — an automated daily slate; the product is only as good as it is current.

## What we say / what we don't
| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "model lean," "edge," "confidence," "no play" | "lock," "guaranteed," "sure thing," "free money" |
| "paper bankroll," "tracked on paper" | "bet," "wager," "place this," "cash out" (as real money) |
| "here's the risk / what breaks it" | anything implying certainty or advice |
| "settled result: W/L/push" | hiding or omitting losing records |

## Launch framing (soft)
Positioned to a receptive first audience, not a mass push. Lead with the **honesty + the real ledger** (the thing no competitor shows). Primary hook = "a pick product that shows its whole record." Keep the volume low; let the product's freshness and honesty do the talking; iterate.

## Required on-site at launch (compliance-of-tone)
Persistent disclaimer (paper-only, not betting advice, no guarantees), Responsible-Use content, honest freshness/status badges, and visible losing records where they exist. Positioning fails if any page reads as real-money advice or certainty.

## Audience channels (for later launch memo)
Candidate first channels favor communities that reward transparency (analytics/dev + sports-analytics spaces) even though the *primary long-term user* is the mainstream fan. Exact channel plan → `launch/` follow-up before the day-of.
