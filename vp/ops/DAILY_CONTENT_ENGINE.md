# Daily Content Engine — the honest Dan-Gamble loop

**By:** Claude (VP) · **2026-07-07** · owned day-to-day by the Social Media Head (per the AI Company Operating Model).
**Goal:** compound an audience with a daily, shareable, *honest* content rhythm — the traction model of @dangambleAI, in GameTime Picks' own no-hype, receipt-first voice. **Nothing auto-posts; every draft goes Head → Yash.**

## F. Daily content schedule (ET)
| Time | Step | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8:00 AM | **Data refresh** | Data Engineer / SOL | fresh odds, props, schedules for today's slate |
| 9:00 AM | **Model board** | Sport Analysts → SOL | Top 10 + per-sport leans + card candidate(s) |
| 10:00 AM | **Founder approval** | **Yash** | approve the card (or no-play); approve the day's angles |
| 10:30 AM | **Public slate drop** | Social Head → Yash | the day's honest slate post (after approval) |
| 12:00 PM | **X + Discord post** | X / Discord Managers → Head → Yash | "today's 3 model leans" + best no-play |
| ~1 hr pregame | **Pregame reminder** | Social Head → Yash | "slate is live" nudge (no hype) |
| Postgame (final) | **Settlement recap** | Soccer/Baseball Analyst → SOL → Head → Yash | honest W/L/pending recap + bankroll update |
| Next 8–9 AM | **Model review** | Sport Analysts | settled-only review; feeds tomorrow's content |

**Rhythm:** morning = generate + approve + drop; midday = amplify; pregame = remind; postgame = the receipt; next morning = learn + repeat. The **settlement recap is the flywheel** — it's the proof no competitor posts.

## Metrics to track (weekly, per `ops/METRICS_SNAPSHOT_TEMPLATE.md`)
Reach/impressions, follows, engagement rate, click-through to Trust Center, which post types land, DAU/returning, and — the honest one — **do posts still lead with the record and the no-play?** Growth that comes from hype is the wrong growth.

## What NOT to say (hard copy rules)
Never: "lock," "guaranteed," "risk-free," "safe," "easy money," "can't lose," "sure thing," "bet this," "place this," or any real-money framing. Never hide a losing day or a lost card. Never imply certainty. Never fabricate a number. Always: "paper-only," "tracked," "not betting advice," "here's what breaks it," and lead with the record.
