# GameTime Picks — Vision & Business Model (one-pager)

**Maintained by:** Claude (VP) · **v1 — 2026-07-06** · grounded in founder decisions ADR-0001…0010

## What it is
GameTime Picks is an **honest, paper-only sports analytics product**: a model compares its own projections to real sportsbook lines, publishes explainable picks with confidence, and tracks *every* result in public. A single canonical bankroll ($100 → real settled record) moves only through official-results settlement, forensically audited. It looks like a sportsbook; it behaves like a research lab.

## Why it's different (the moat)
**Provable honesty.** Most pick products hide their misses and sell certainty. We show the 0–17 records as plainly as the winners, never move money except on official finals, and never fabricate. That discipline is technically enforced (gates, md5 guards, idempotence) — it's hard to copy and it's exactly what earns trust. **Trust is the moat; the model is intentionally simple.**

## Who it's for
- **Primary:** sports fans who want simple, trustworthy, model-backed picks without studying the math. The bar is "know what to bet in 30 seconds," honestly.
- **Secondary:** evaluators — recruiters, investors, collaborators — assessing the product and the AI operating system behind it.

## The journey (short → long term)
1. **Now → launch (July 10):** polished, paper-only, daily-fresh product that builds trust. Soft launch.
2. **3 months:** automated daily slates, visible model performance, strong flagships (Bank Builder, Top 10, Mr. Dub), a growing audience. Measured by freshness, trust, daily active usage, settled performance.
3. **6–12 months:** scale into a real sports analytics company — audience-led, credibility-led.

## Flagships
Bank Builder (the paper bankroll ladder — the proof), Top 10 board (the daily "what's good" cross-sport read), Mr. Dub (the honest ledger surface). World Cup drives today; **MLB becomes the primary sport post-tournament**, with NFL/NHL to follow on the same pipeline.

## Business model (deliberately deferred)
**No monetization now.** Build trust, traffic, and a track record first. **No sportsbook affiliate revenue, ever** — it would quietly compromise the honesty claim. Later, honest paths: premium analytics, subscriptions, and tools (e.g. a paper bankroll simulator, saved slips, deeper model reads). The order is non-negotiable: **credibility → audience → product depth → revenue.**

## Guardrails (what we will never do)
Take real-money bets · use guarantee/"lock"/certainty language · fabricate odds, scores, or records · move money on anything but official finals · feature a product's honesty record less prominently than its wins.

## The one-sentence pitch
*"Model-backed sports picks with nothing hidden — every pick explained, every result tracked, all on paper."*
