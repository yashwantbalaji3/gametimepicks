## Three roles, kept separate

| Role | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Official GameTimePicks forecast** | The canonical frozen snapshot: Elo winner side (N2) + v0.1 player medians (N1) | The official panel never changes when a replay runs, a run is chosen, or a what-if is opened |
| **B. NBA v1 Challenger** | `nba-sim-v1-coherent` | Model evaluation and comparison only. Never overwrites, rounds into or replaces an official number |
| **C. Research Possession Simulation** | `nba-possession-sim-v0` | Drives Theater playback. Every play, score, clock state, possession and lineup comes from the stored event log, and the box score is the fold of that log. Not official, not promoted, not counted toward validation or any product record |

## Scope

- **Where.** The public route `/nba/simulation/[eventId]` (#1167), only for games with a verified pre-tip official snapshot and stored possession runs built from as-of-tip inputs.
- **What the user gets:**
  - four-quarter animated playback, possession by possession;
  - a synchronised clock and scoreboard;
  - scoring events, substitutions, and play-by-play;
  - player contributions and overtime;
  - pause, resume, restart and speed controls;
  - final team and player box scores;
  - an official-versus-simulated comparison;
  - clearly labelled alternative runs.
- **What stays withheld (preregistration §13):**
  - official win probability, official team score and ranges;
  - odds, lines, edges, picks and rankings.
  - No Theater output feeds Bank Builder, Moonshot, Parlay Lab or Smart Betslip. The registry stays `HISTORICAL_ONLY`.

## Required on-screen labelling

**Primary label:** **Research Possession Simulation — Unvalidated**.

The note beside it must say:
- the replay is generated by an independent research simulation engine;
- it is not the official GameTimePicks forecast;
- one replay is one possible modelled outcome;
- a different simulated score does not mean the official forecast changed;
- the engine's predictive superiority has not been established.

Nothing may imply a real or live basketball game.

## The flag

There are two acts, both performed by the release owner only:
1. A reviewed commit sets `NBA_THEATER_PUBLIC_ENABLED = true` and `NBA_THEATER_PUBLIC_GATE.decisionRecorded = true`, citing this file.
2. The Production build sets `GTP_NBA_THEATER_PUBLIC=1`.

The route gates its imports on an inline `process.env.GTP_NBA_THEATER_PUBLIC === "1"` literal, so the flag-OFF build contains no Theater chunk, pack or possession marker. This was verified in CI on #1167 at `d5b2861273`.

**Input cutoff (release owner, 2026-10-21, under NBA-CC requirement 1 "identical cutoffs").**
- A possession run may use only inputs captured at or before the **official forecast's freeze instant** for that game. "Before tip" is not enough.
- Why: the official-versus-simulated comparison must not give the research replay later information than the official forecast had.
- Effect on 10-20: 401909089 and 401909090 used inputs from 15:05Z / 17:49Z, after the 14:17Z freeze. Those two games stay closed unless their runs are regenerated from freeze-time inputs.
- Labels and copy (T1), and the official panel showing exactly the lane's players (amendment B), are done at `3d614189ba` [reported].

**Permitted markers in the flag-ON export** (G8): the engine id `nba-possession-sim-v0`, the Theater pack schema, and its data attributes, on `/nba/simulation/<id>/` only. Everything in checklist §5 stays forbidden.

## Rollback

Set `NBA_THEATER_PUBLIC_ENABLED = false`, or unset `GTP_NBA_THEATER_PUBLIC`, and rebuild. Roll back on any of these:
- a printed official number that differs from its receipt;
- a withheld item that becomes visible;
- a replay shown as official;
- an export leak;
- wrong game identity;
- an event log that does not reconcile with its box score.

## Not authorized here
- Promoting `nba-possession-sim-v0` (or v1) to the official NBA model.
- Any NBA probability, team score, range, price or pick.
- Any product use of Theater output.
