Head reviews and merges drafts into one approval packet; **Yash approves** every post and the brand
direction. Nothing auto-posts. Part of the [AI Company Operating System](AI_COMPANY_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md).*

## The approval flow (draft → Head → Yash)
1. **Facts** — the Social Media Head pulls the day's honest facts from the [Sports Ops Daily Brief](SPORT_STANDUP_TEMPLATE.md) (record, notable calls, milestones) and assigns angles.
2. **Draft** — each channel manager writes drafts using the [Social Draft Template](SOCIAL_DRAFT_TEMPLATE.md) — status **DRAFT**.
3. **Head review** — the Head runs the responsible-copy check and merges drafts — status **HEAD-REVIEWED**.
4. **Founder approval** — the Head sends ONE approval packet to Yash; Yash approves — status **YASH-APPROVED**. Only then may a post go out (manually, until posting tooling is built and approved).

## Responsible-copy rules (every draft)
**Draft-only · Head-reviewed · Yash-approved · no auto-post · no hype · no real-money claims · no guarantees · do not hide losses.** The product is paper-only educational analytics — never imply real-money wagering, never promise profit, always show the honest record (a losing day is shown as a losing day).

## What Social does NOT do (yet)
No auto-posting, no scheduling code, no publishing — those require tooling **and** founder sign-off that don't
exist yet. Until then, everything is a draft that a human posts after Yash approves. Brand direction is a
founder call.
