## 1. Executive summary
- **Winner: miss.** Both models favoured Dallas:
  - World Model V2: 64.4% Dallas.
  - The incumbent published forecast: 61.8% Dallas.
  - The pregame market was more confident still, at about 80.5% no-vig.

  Tampa Bay won 24–16. A 35% outcome happening is not evidence of miscalibration, and one game cannot measure calibration.
- **Score:** TB's 24 points were exactly the median of TB's simulated points (51st percentile). DAL's 16 were in the lower tail: 8.9th percentile, outside the 80% interval of 17–40. The margin (home −8) and the total (40) were inside their 80% intervals, at the 16th and 18th percentiles.
- **Players:**
  - 36 player-market forecasts were graded; 6 were not graded because there is no box-score line (NO_LINE).
  - The 80% intervals covered 28 of 36. By family:
    - passing yards 2/2
    - rushing yards 5/6
    - receiving yards 9/14
    - receptions 12/14
  - The biggest misses were Bucky Irving's 165 rushing yards (99.5th percentile), CeeDee Lamb's 9 receiving yards (2.5th) and George Pickens's 130 receiving yards (94.1st).
- **Volume vs efficiency:**
  - **Volume was represented.** In the worlds where TB won by 8 or more, the simulated volumes moved in the direction they did in reality, and TB's 39 carries and DAL's 13 sit inside the conditional 80% bands.
  - **Rushing efficiency was not.** Irving's 7.86 yards per carry on 21 carries happened in 0.17% of the worlds where he had 20 or more carries. In 2013–2025 it happened in 1.79% of such games.

  So the upper tail of per-carry yardage is about ten times thinner than history. This is a **supported hypothesis** for NFL-003, not a confirmed defect and not a reason to refit on this game.
- **Sportsbook thresholds:** World Model V2 published **no pregame over/under selections**. Line outcomes below are therefore descriptive only, not pick wins or losses.
- **Grading and settlement:** as of this report, TB @ DAL has **not** been graded or settled in the repository. Both run only inside the NFL event window, and the Friday 13:00 UTC run had not started at 13:37 UTC; this scheduler is always late. Re-running the same pipeline in a scratch copy of `main` grades the game correctly. Section 16 has the details.

## 4. World Model V2 vs the incumbent: what is and is not independent
World Model V2 draws its **scores from the incumbent's own margin and total heads**: margin mean +5, sigma 12.875; total mean 52, sigma 13.27. So the two are **not independent game models**:
- **V2's win chance** is the share of simulated games each side won, overtime included.
- **The incumbent's win chance** comes from a separate Elo win head.

The differences between them are in the win head and in V2's coherent player worlds, not in the score distribution.
