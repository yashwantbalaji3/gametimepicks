## 7. Classification of the issue
- **Risk-classification (methodology) bug** — LOW is assigned purely by the combined-odds band (`PUBLIC_RISK_SECTION_SPECS.low.max_am_exclusive = 300`), with **no per-leg form (L10≥80%) or odds floor**. This lets plus-money / weak-form legs into LOW.
- **Data-source bug** — NBA game logs are Regular-Season-only (provider hardcodes `season_type_all_star="Regular Season"`), so playoff form never surfaces → stale L10.
- **Missing policy guard** — no "fail-closed on untrusted/stale form" for LOW.
- **Stale generated data** — June 5's publicRiskSections was generated by the old code; **fixing June 5 itself requires regeneration** (paid). The code fix applies automatically to future slates.
