# Friends beta — invitation kit (cohort `friends-beta-2026-09`)

For the founder to send personally to up to five close friends. Nothing here collects a name or an
address; testers are referred to as T1–T5 in `FEEDBACK_LOG.md`.

## The message (copy, adjust the greeting)

> Hey — I've been building **GameTimePicks** (gametimepicks.yashwantbalaji.com), a free sports-analytics
> site: game simulations, model reads, and two paper-money "ladder" challenges. No real money, no sign-up.
>
> Could you use it for the next two weeks — even a couple of minutes a day — and tell me what's confusing,
> broken, or useful? The easiest way to send feedback is the **Contact support** link at the bottom of any
> page.
>
> **Please note:** the site's Terms of Use and Privacy Notice are still under legal review and are not yet
> published. The site takes no bets, handles no money, sets no cookies, and never asks for your name or
> email. It's for adults 18 and older in the US.

## What to try (a two-minute daily loop)

1. **Morning (by 8:00 AM ET):** open the home page or **Today** — is today's slate there and readable?
2. **Pick one game and open its simulation.** Does the report make sense without explanation?
3. **Bank Builder and Moonshot:** can you tell what step each ladder is on and what happens if a card wins?
4. **Results:** does the record feel honest and easy to check?

## Four questions to ask at the end

1. What did you open most, and why?
2. What confused you the first time?
3. Did anything look broken or wrong (dates, numbers, missing pictures)?
4. Would you come back tomorrow without being asked? What would make you?

## Stop conditions (from the cohort contract)

Pause the beta if anything shows a wrong settled result, a money figure that does not reconcile, or
a page that breaks on a phone — fix first, then resume.
