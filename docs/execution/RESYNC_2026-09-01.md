The charter's P0 item: *"one owner must reconcile pending count, settled count, exposure, and card

### What was false

Four artifacts described Moonshot and no two agreed, and the public page asserted something none of
them supported:

| Source | Last written | Says |
|---|---|---|
| `moonshot-lane/active.json` | 2026-08-17 | `status: "active"`; lanes A and B each hold a step-1 card, six legs, every result `null` |

| `mr-dub/portfolio.json` → `.moonshot` | 2026-07-07 | `status: "stopped"`, record 0-1, pending 0 — and the card it names is a **World Cup** parlay |
| **`/moonshot`** (live) | — | "Two independent longshot cards published **daily**", "Lifetime paper record **0–1**", "**0 Pending** · 1 Settled" |
| **`/mr-dub`** (live) | — | "Independent **daily** high-volatility longshot cards … settles from official box scores" |

Four public claims were false at once: daily publication, the smaller of two settled counts presented
as "lifetime", zero pending beside two open cards, and a settlement promise for cards that cannot be
settled.

### Why the product cannot publish, and why it cannot settle

Two separate breaks:

1. **Nothing generates it.** `activate-moonshot-candidates.mjs` is a dry-run decision tool whose
   `--apply` is *deliberately* refused — the Mr. Dub ledger models a single active card, so activating
   two concurrent lanes would mis-account exposure. That refusal is correct and was written on purpose.
   No workflow references it. `lineup-aware-refresh.yml` can place a Moonshot card but is
   dispatch-only (its cron was a one-off World Cup window, since expired and removed), World Cup
   scoped, and `AUTO_PLACE_MOONSHOT` defaults false.

2. **The wired settler cannot see the open cards.** `nightly-settle` runs
   `settle-paper-product-cards.mjs`, which walks `data/internal/product-cards/` — **a directory that
   does not exist**. The Aug-17 cards were written by a manual restart script straight into the lane
   artifact and were never registered as product cards. They also carry no game identity: not one of
   the six legs has a `gamePk`, so even registered they could not be joined to a box score.

So the cards are **abandoned**, not pending. Calling them pending promises a settlement no code path
can deliver.

### A liveness predicate that could never be false

`buildDailyPortfolio` synthesizes two empty Moonshot placeholders — legs `[]`, $0, status "awaiting" —
for **every** date, verified against a date seven months out. The page tested liveness with
`cards.filter(product === "moonshot").length > 0`, which was therefore `true` on every day the site has
ever rendered. That constant-true predicate is what lit **"Day 1 · LIVE"** on the ladder and promised
"settles overnight from official results" for cards that do not exist. Liveness now requires legs: a
card with no legs is not a card.

### What shipped

- **`app/src/lib/products/moonshot-state.mjs`** — one pure owner. Derives the lifecycle
  (`PUBLISHED` / `SETTLING` / `ABANDONED` / `NOT_GENERATING` / `STALE` / `RETIRED` / `UNKNOWN`), the
  open cards and their settleability, the stranded stake, and the public sentence. It **reports** the
  ledger disagreement rather than averaging it away, and `displayRecord` gives surfaces with room for
  one figure a single answer (the settlement log) so they cannot drift apart again.
- **`/moonshot`** — derived note, honest "Not running" chip, no counts chips (neither number can
  honestly be a single figure here), and a **reconciliation section** stating last-published, settled
  record, open cards, stranded stake and every stored disagreement in the open.
- **`/mr-dub`** — the same owner, so the two surfaces cannot diverge.
- **`moonshot-lane-tracker`** — stops trusting the artifact's self-declared `status`; a `running={false}`
  product shows "Stopped" and "No new cards are being published".
- **Guards** — 22 tests, including two that pin the constants against the real workflow directory and
  the real product-cards directory, and one that scans **rendered copy only** (comments blanked
  line-preservingly: the first version failed on the docblock that quotes the old claim to explain it,
  and a guard that cannot tell a defect from its explanation pushes you to delete the explanation).
