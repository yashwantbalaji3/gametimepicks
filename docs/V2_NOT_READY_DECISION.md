## 2. What data is missing

- **MLB batter/pitcher handedness** and **confirmed-starter** fields are not
  present on graded optimizer legs → those features cannot be validated at all
  from data on disk (not just under-sampled — entirely absent).
- **NBA board recent-form** — no `app/public/data/nba/boards/` directory, and
  NBA series ordering is unverified, so NBA legs fail closed for L5/L10.
- **World Cup** pregame data — not in the repo; out of scope.
- For the *recency/quality* gates that DO have data (L5 5/5, Low), the missing
  ingredient is simply **more settled slates** (volume), not new fields.

## 3. What sample size is needed

- **≥ 40 decided legs** in each gated bucket (L5 5/5 and Low-eligible). Today:
  L5 5/5 = **31**, Low = **23**. Roughly **3–8 more settled MLB slates** should
  push both past 40 (the public era has been adding ~3–5 L5-5/5 and ~2–4 Low
  legs per slate). The learning-loop floor for any public claim is additionally
  **≥ 25 decisive picks per market, 100+ slips per lane, 200+ overall** with
  **out-of-sample** confirmation (`docs/MODEL_LEARNING_LOOP.md`).
- Reaching N≥40 is **necessary but not sufficient**: the bucket must then show a
  95% CI lower bound above the model-selected baseline AND an out-of-sample beat
  of the **de-vigged** market (not raw implied), confirmed across day-folds.

## 4. What to test next

1. **Keep accumulating slates.** After each nightly settle, re-run:
   ```bash
   cd app
   npx tsx scripts/audit-v2-end-to-end-readiness.mjs --write-report
   npx tsx scripts/shadow-parlay-methodology-v2.mjs --write-report
   ```
   Commit the refreshed reports as a tiny docs-only change (per
   `docs/METHODOLOGY_V2_POST_SETTLE_CHECKLIST.md`). Watch L5 5/5 and Low climb
   toward 40.
2. **De-vig the market baseline.** The recalibration audit currently compares
   against *raw* implied prob (includes vig). Re-run the "beats market OOS"
   test against a **de-vigged** two-sided baseline before drawing the final
   recalibration conclusion — recalibration ties raw implied today, so the
   de-vigged bar is even higher.
3. **Resolve the #241 cap-vs-target decision** (raise caps vs accept fewer
   cards) — this is an operator product decision, independent of evidence.
4. **If/when L5 5/5 and Low both clear 40** with a CI lower bound above
   baseline AND a de-vigged OOS beat: only then promote a *single* narrow
   feature (likely Low-only) behind a feature flag, with a one-PR rollback, and
   **STOP for operator approval** before wiring.

## 5. Why June 4 / the current slate stays current-live

- **No v2 feature beats the de-vigged market.** The only durable signal is the
  market price itself; the model's own quality signals (`edgePct`,
  `confidence`) are anti-/non-predictive. There is no validated edge to wire.
- **The promising buckets are under-sampled** (N=31 / N=23 < 40). Their high
  hit rates (77% / 83%) sit inside wide CIs and could regress; promoting on them
  now would be an unvalidated claim.
- **Several features have no data at all** (handedness, confirmed starter, NBA,
  World Cup) — nothing to launch.
- Promoting anything today would violate the hard rules: no public win-rate
  claim without sample + OOS confirmation; no `edgePct`/`confidence` as quality
  signals; no padding. The current-live model already runs honestly within
  those rules.

**Therefore the current-live methodology remains in production. v2 stays
shadow-only and observational. Re-evaluate after more slates settle.**
