# Multi-Sport Capability Audit — GameTimePicks

_Audit date: 2026-07-22. Method: artifact-backed — every claim below cites an exact route, script, workflow, data dir, or JSON artifact that was opened and inspected (dates read from inside the JSON, game/leg counts verified). No code or data was modified except this document._

## Summary

MLB is the only genuinely production-grade sport: real current data flows end-to-end (board `app/public/data/mlb/boards/2026-07-22.json` = 17 games / 678 leans, 10k-run simulations, team-markets, settled leans through 2026-07-21, forward-only pregame research capture through 2026-07-22, plus active cron automation). Every other sport is either off-season, schedule-only scaffolding, retired/archived, or fail-closed internal research. The canonical registry `app/src/lib/sports-coverage.ts` marks **MLB and NBA as `level: "full"`**, but that NBA designation is aspirational — NBA is out of season and its data pipeline (the repo's default/root `app/public/data/boards/`) has emitted only empty `ScheduleUnavailable` boards since the 2026-06-13 Finals. The richest _dedicated_ non-MLB infrastructure belongs to **UFC** (schedule + odds + a 2,695-fighter stats DB + grading + a v1/v2 prediction engine + 8 workflows), but it fail-closes to internal-only because no historical backtest has been run (0/150 clean rows). World Cup is explicitly **retired/archived** and removed from the registry and nav. NHL, IPL/cricket, WNBA, and non-WC soccer (EPL/UCL/MLS) are schedule-only or scaffold. NFL and NCAAB do not exist beyond a single type-enum literal.

## Per-Sport Detail

### MLB — PRODUCTION_READY
Fully current, end-to-end. `app/public/data/mlb/boards/2026-07-22.json` was opened and holds 17 games / 678 leans generated `2026-07-22T15:22Z`; matching same-day `player-props/2026-07-22.json`, `game-simulations/2026-07-22.json` (carries `runCount`, `modelVersion`, `simulationVersion`), and `team-markets/2026-07-22.json` (real bookmaker lines) exist. Settlement is live: `results/settled_leans.jsonl` has 19,465 rows with the last entry dated 2026-07-21, plus `comparison_report_2026-07-21.json`. The forward-only research pipeline is current — `data/internal/mlb/pregame-archive/market-snapshots/2026-07-22/` exists alongside snapshots, freezes, manifests, and research-observations. Automation is real and scheduled: `mlb-daily-production.yml` (cron `15 14 * * *`), `mlb-pregame-capture.yml` (5 crons), and `nightly-settle.yml`. 56 MLB scripts under `app/scripts`. **Justification:** real current data + projections + 10k simulation + settlement + research archive + active cron automation. Honesty caveat (not a capability blocker): `app/src/lib/mlb/model-calibration-status.ts` records all four modeled markets as NOT market-beating and the product is publicly framed "public beta."

### NBA — HISTORICAL_ONLY
NBA is the repo's _default/root_ sport — `app/public/data/board.json`, `slate.json`, `meta.json`, and `boards/` are NBA (the `failureReason` fields cite `stats.nba.com` timeouts, `nbaScheduleSource:"unavailable"`). The registry `sports-coverage.ts` marks it `level:"full"` (sharing `/projections`, `/parlay-lab`, `/results` with MLB), and the `/nba` hub has the full board/parlays/power/results scaffold. But every current board is empty: `boards/2026-07-25.json` is `dataMode:ScheduleUnavailable` with zero games, and the newest board that actually contains a game is `boards/2026-06-13.json` (1 game — Finals). `nba/team_projections/` is stale to 2026-05-21 and the only workflow, `nba-market-probe.yml`, is `workflow_dispatch` only. `sports/page.tsx` renders the NBA tile as "Board: Finals / Plays: pending." **Justification:** a genuine full pipeline that ran during the playoffs but now has only past/archived data and no current season — the "full" label is aspirational until the season returns.

### NHL — SCAFFOLD_ONLY
`/nhl/page.tsx` self-documents the state: "NHL ships in `providerPending`... the model board, parlays, and audit are honestly empty. We do not fabricate projections." Only `app/public/data/nhl/schedule/` exists (2026-05-17 through 2026-05-24, stale), loaded from the free NHL API; `board/[date]/page.tsx` shows "No NHL data" when a date isn't on disk. The route carries the same board/parlays/power/results tabs as MLB but they are empty shells — no odds, projection, simulation, or settlement pipeline was ever built, and there are no NHL scripts or workflows. **Justification:** routes/tabs exist but no odds/projection data ever flowed; the only real artifact (schedule) is stale.

### Soccer (EPL / Champions League / MLS, non-World-Cup) — SCAFFOLD_ONLY
There is no `/soccer`, `/epl`, `/mls`, or `/champions-league` route and no `app/public/data/soccer|epl|mls` dir. In `sports-coverage.ts`, EPL is `level:"coming-soon"` (empty links, no data) and MLS is `level:"schedule"`. The only current soccer artifact is the MLS schedule baked into `app/src/lib/event-schedules.ts` (`MLS_SCHEDULE` rangeEnd `2026-07-22`), surfaced solely on `/events`. An internal FIFA/Poisson soccer engine exists (`data/internal/world-cup/projection-engine/2026-07-14.json`, `backtest-soccer-2022-wc*.mjs`, `build-internal-soccer-projections.mjs`) but is research-only and unvalidated (N insufficient). Champions League has no presence. **Justification:** no public soccer product exists apart from a schedule snapshot; the projection engine is internal/experimental.

### World Cup — HISTORICAL_ONLY
Explicitly retired. World Cup is **absent from `sports-coverage.ts`** and excluded from nav (`nav.tsx`: "The 2026 World Cup is complete: it is NOT an active nav destination"). `/world-cup/page.tsx` fail-closes its freshness anchor to null so a completed tournament never reads "Live today," and renders an archive badge. Data is rich but historical: `world-cup/projections/latest.json` is stamped 2026-07-21 but `matchCount:0`; the newest real match settlement is `world-cup/settlement/official-scores-2026-07-07.json`. `world-cup-closeout.test.mjs` enforces the archive-only state, and 30+ WC test files exist (now all archival). `world-cup-odds.yml` is `workflow_dispatch` only. **Justification:** complete tournament, archived by design, no current matches.

### IPL / Cricket — SCAFFOLD_ONLY
`/ipl/page.tsx` mirrors NHL: `providerPending`, schedule from ESPN's free cricket scoreboard, "Model board pending a stable per-player stats source," "do not fabricate projections." `ipl/schedule/` is stale (to 2026-05-24) and the board/parlays/power/results tabs are empty. Separately, `app/public/data/cricket/` holds a single-date board/context/player-projections set (all `2026-05-26.json`) but there is **no `/cricket` route** — that data is orphaned. `sports-coverage.ts` lists IPL at `level:"schedule"`. **Justification:** route scaffold with stale schedule-only data, no projection pipeline, and an orphaned cricket board with no surface.

### UFC — RESEARCH_ONLY
The richest _dedicated_ non-MLB stack: `ufc/schedule-latest.json` (UFC 329, 2026-07-11, ESPN MMA), `ufc/odds-latest.json` (20 bouts, the-odds-api, h2h only), a 2,695-fighter / 17,402-fight stats DB (`fighters-latest.json`, `readiness-latest.json` `providerStatus`), grading connected (1,519 final bouts), a v1+v2 prediction engine (`app/src/lib/ufc/ufc-prediction-engine.ts`, `ufc-predictions-v2.tsx`), internal projections (`data/internal/ufc/projections-internal-latest.json`), and 8 `ufc-*.yml` workflows. But it **fail-closes**: `readiness-latest.json` reports `projectionsReady:false`, `parlayReady:false`, `backtestReady:false` with `publicLevel:"grading-internal"` and blocker "no historical backtest yet" (0/150 clean rows); `data/internal/ufc/backfill-status.json` status is "not-started." All 8 UFC workflows are `workflow_dispatch` only (no cron), and `sports-coverage.ts` lists UFC at `level:"schedule"`. The last event was 11 days before the audit. **Justification:** a real internal projection+grading pipeline exists but nothing publishes — the public `/ufc` surface is a fight card plus gated projections pending a backtest.

### NFL — UNSUPPORTED
The only reference in the entire app source is the type literal `"nfl"` in `app/src/lib/multi-sport-report/schema.ts` (`ReportSport = "mlb" | "soccer" | "ufc" | "nba" | "nfl"`) and its validator. No data dir, route, script, workflow, or real test. **Justification:** a placeholder enum value with nothing behind it.

### WNBA — HISTORICAL_ONLY
In `sports-coverage.ts` at `level:"schedule"`, surfaced only via `/events`. Its schedule is a hard-coded snapshot in `event-schedules.ts` (`WNBA_SCHEDULE`, 2026-06-02 through 2026-06-05 — ~7 weeks stale) retrieved from ESPN once (`ESPN_RETRIEVED_AT` constants). No dedicated route, no odds, projections, or automation. **Justification:** only a stale baked schedule snapshot with no live pipeline.

### NCAAB — UNSUPPORTED
No references anywhere — zero scripts, routes, data dirs, workflows, or tests match `ncaab`. **Justification:** nothing built.
